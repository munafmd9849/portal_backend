Interview Process

📝
1
Round 1: Aptitude Test
Initial screening test covering quantitative, logical, and verbal reasoning. This round assesses your fundamental problem-solving abilities and analytical thinking.
Next: Round 2: Technical Interview
💻
2
Round 2: Technical Interview
In-depth discussion of technical skills, problem-solving, and domain knowledge. Be prepared to demonstrate your coding abilities and technical expertise.
Next: Round 3: HR Interview
👔
3
Round 3: HR Interview
Evaluation of cultural fit, communication skills, and career aspirations. This round focuses on understanding your personality, values, and long-term goals.
Next: Round 4: Group Discussion
👥
4
Round 4: Group Discussion
Assessment of teamwork, leadership, and communication abilities. You'll participate in group activities to demonstrate your collaborative skills.
Next: Round 5: Final Decision
⚖️
5
Round 5: Final Decision
Selection committee review and final decision making. The hiring team evaluates all candidates and makes the final selection.
Next: OFFER
🎉
6
OFFER
Formal job offer extended to selected candidates. Congratulations! You'll receive the official offer letter with compensation and benefits details.
Next: Onboarding
🚀
7
Onboarding
Final Step
Orientation and integration process for new hires. Welcome to the team! You'll go through orientation, training, and meet your colleagues.
